What is EXIF data?

EXIF stands for Exchangeable Image File Format. It's information saved inside a photo: the camera and lens, exposure settings, when it was taken, and sometimes its GPS location. This information is called metadata. Not every image contains all of these details.

Checking your photo settings can help you figure out why a subject looks blurry or which lens you used. You can also check for saved GPS coordinates before sharing a photo. The viewer reads IPTC and XMP metadata too, including captions, keywords, and creator credits.
